Water Level Monitoring

Know your water level without opening the tank.

A removable sensor mount measures the level from inside the tank lid and sends the reading to your phone.

Join the waitlist See how it works
The problem

Checking a tank should not mean lifting the lid.

For many homes, the tank is the real water supply. But the level is usually checked by walking outside, opening the tank, and guessing from what you can see.

That is easy to forget, awkward to do, and often discovered too late.

The solution

A removable sensor system for the tank lid.

The sensor sits beneath the lid and reads the distance to the water surface. The electronics are held separately, away from the water path, and the level is sent wirelessly to your phone.

  • No drilling.
  • No tools.
  • No permanent tank modification.

Engineering

The physical design matters.

  • Sensor position The ultrasonic sensor faces the water surface from the lid area, so it can take readings without touching the water.
  • Mounting The current mount uses a removable support beneath the lid instead of drilling into the tank.
  • Electronics The control box holds the power and wireless hardware outside the water path.
  • Fit testing Tank shape, lid geometry, signal range, and battery life are being checked before the pilot batch.
Product status

Pilot testing is focused on mount fit, wireless range, battery life, and day-to-day use.

Do I need to drill into my tank?

No permanent tank modification is the goal of the current design. The mount is being developed around a removable under-lid support.

Will it work with my tank?

Fit depends on the tank and lid shape. Waitlist signups can share their tank type so compatibility can be checked before a pilot install.

How long does the battery last?

Battery life is currently being tested. The final number should come from real use, not a guess.

Will it need Wi-Fi?

The current direction is wireless phone monitoring. Range and connection details are being tested, especially for tanks located away from the house.

When can I get one?

A pilot batch is planned after testing. Waitlist signups will receive availability and pricing details first.
